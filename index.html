FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? WhatsApp or email us.

Which areas do you cover?

JURTO delivers goods across Chennai.

How do I know the price?

The app shows the fare for every vehicle before you book. There are no surprise charges after the trip.

How can I pay?

Cash, JURTO Wallet, UPI, card or netbanking. Online payments go through Razorpay.

Can I book for later?

Yes. You can schedule a pickup up to 7 days ahead.

Can I deliver to more than one place?

Yes. You can add up to 3 extra stops in one trip.

How are my goods kept safe?

Drivers are verified with their licence, RC book and ID. Your goods are handed over only after you share the pickup OTP, and you can track the trip live.

How do I become a JURTO driver?

Sign up in the JURTO Partner app with your mobile number, add your licence, RC book and ID proof, and go online once you are verified.
